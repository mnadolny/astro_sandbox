Speed of Light Foundation · theme for review

Speed of Light theme

Final draft for review October 8, 2026

The Speed of Light theme uses the two colors in the Foundation's logo: ASTRO blue does the structural work, and raspberry is the accent. Donate is the one solid raspberry button, so it stands out on every page. Body and headings use Nunito, with body text at 18px for easier reading.

Changes

What changed from the dev's proposal

Everything else in the dev's palette carries over as written.

Green removed #4F7E2A is no longer in the theme. Pink and green sat on opposite sides of the color wheel at the same strength and clashed wherever they met.
Secondary button →blue outline White with a brand-blue border and text. Fills brand blue on hover.
Donate button →#BA1F7B Raspberry with white text (5.88:1). Darkens to #931961 on hover. It's the only solid raspberry button.
Raspberry tint →#F8EAF2 A true pale pink in place of the beige-looking tint, so accent panels read as part of the raspberry family.
Search button →blue icon Brand-blue icon with no fill, darkening to dark blue on hover. The pink fill is gone from the header.
Body text size 16px → 18px Body text is 18px with a 1.6 line height (1.5 in cards and panels), and small text never drops below 15px. Many of the site's readers are older.
H3 and card titles 20px regular H3s and content-card titles are 20px at regular weight, not extra bold. Card descriptions and due dates below them are 15px.
Lighter accent role defined The ticket listed #F2B1D7 without a job. It's now the text-highlight underlay and the hover state for light-pink controls.

Typography

Nunito everywhere, system mono for code

One family carries both headings and body. Body text is 18px with a 1.6 line height, because many of the site's readers are older. Cards and panels use 1.5. Small text never goes below 15px. Heading sizes are illustrative.
