## Key structural changes from current site

| Current | Proposed | Why |
|---------|----------|-----|
| Practice Support + Provider Resources (separate) | Practice (merged) | Same audience, artificially split by dept |
| About ASTRO (footer only) | About & Join (primary nav) | Invisible to new visitors |
| Membership (footer only) | About & Join (primary nav) | Critical conversion path |
| Medical Students / ARRO / Early Career (footer) | About & Join > Career Pathways | Buried — now first-class nav |
| Annual Meeting (disconnected microsite) | Connect > Meetings (main nav shell) | Brand consistency |
| RTAnswers (under Provider Resources) | Utility nav > For Patients | Patient content in patient pathway |
| Physician Networking (under Shareable Resources) | Connect > Community | Wrong section |
| ROhub (always-visible utility nav) | Welcome Back dropdown | Logged-in feature only |
| Join ASTRO (secondary bar) | Become a Member (utility nav) | More prominent, clearer label |
